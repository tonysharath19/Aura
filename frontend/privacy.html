AURA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AURA may collect, use, store and protect information when you use the AURA website, account, wellness dashboard and AI features.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to users of AURA's website, registration flow, wellness dashboard, AI assistant, administrative interfaces and related digital services.

In this Policy, "AURA", "we", "our" and "us" refer to [AURA LEGAL / COMPANY NAME].

2. Information We May Collect

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication/session information

Profile and wellness information

  • Age and gender, where provided
  • Height and weight
  • Body measurements and calculated wellness metrics
  • Fitness goals
  • Activity level
  • Sleep preferences and sleep records
  • Water-intake baseline and hydration records
  • Nutrition preferences
  • Food preferences and intolerances
  • Wellness conditions or information you choose to provide
  • Workout completion records
  • Nutrition completion records
  • Hydration logs
  • Sleep logs
  • Progress, streak and achievement information

AI interaction information

Questions and messages you send to AURA AI may be processed in order to generate responses. Chat history may be stored so that previous conversations can be shown when you reopen the AURA assistant.

3. Information We Do Not Intend to Provide to AURA AI

AURA's AI wellness assistant is designed not to use unnecessary account-security information such as your password for generating responses.

AURA should also avoid sending unnecessary identifying information such as your email address or phone number to the AI model when that information is not required for the requested wellness response.

The exact technical processing implemented in production should be reviewed whenever the AI integration changes.

4. How We Use Information

AURA may use information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you
  • Build and personalize wellness plans
  • Calculate wellness targets
  • Track workouts, nutrition, hydration and sleep
  • Calculate progress, points and streaks
  • Unlock achievements
  • Generate wellness insights
  • Answer your AI wellness questions
  • Maintain chat history
  • Improve reliability, security and performance
  • Investigate abuse or security incidents
  • Comply with applicable law

5. Personalization & AI

AURA may use selected wellness information to personalize AI-generated recommendations.

Depending on the feature, this may include information such as activity, goals, sleep, hydration, nutrition and workout context.

AURA does not intend to use passwords or authentication secrets as content for wellness recommendations.

6. Health, Sexual Health & Reproductive Information

AURA may allow users to ask educational questions about human biology, sexual health, masturbation, contraception, reproduction, fertility, menstruation, pregnancy, sexual function and related topics.

Information a user voluntarily provides in these areas may be processed to provide the requested service.

Users should avoid entering information that is not necessary for the service.

7. How Information Is Stored

AURA may use third-party infrastructure providers for authentication, databases, application hosting and related technical services.

The current production architecture may use Supabase for authentication and database functionality. The exact infrastructure configuration may change as AURA evolves.

8. Service Providers & AI Providers

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf for functions such as cloud infrastructure, authentication, database storage, application hosting, security, monitoring and AI processing.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their relevant services and subject to the contractual and technical arrangements applicable to the service.

9. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

AURA may use browser storage, cookies, session tokens or similar technologies to maintain authentication, remember preferences, preserve chat state and support application functionality.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and stored website data.

10. Data Security

AURA uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong unique password and protect access to your device and account.

11. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, protect security, resolve disputes, comply with legal requirements and fulfill other legitimate operational purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.
